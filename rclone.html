rclone access to my Google Drive — November 2026

rclone is the name of a Google OAuth client I created for my own use. It lets rclone, the open-source file synchronization program, connect to my Google Drive from my computer.

What it does

It keeps one folder of my Google Drive, the library of papers, references and notes I use for my mathematics, in sync with a copy on my computer. rclone runs on my own machine, connects directly to Google Drive, and copies changes in both directions. No server sits in between, and the files go nowhere else.

Who uses it

Only me. The client is not offered to anyone else and connects only to my own Google account.

Privacy

How it handles Google user data is set out in its privacy policy.
