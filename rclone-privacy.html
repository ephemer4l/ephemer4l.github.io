Privacy policy of the rclone client — effective 17 November 2026

This policy covers the Google OAuth client named rclone, which I, Efe İzbudak, created to let rclone, the open-source synchronization program, reach my own Google Drive from my computer. I am its only user. Its homepage is ephemer4l.me/rclone.html.

Data it accesses

The client requests the Google Drive scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive), which lets rclone list, read, create, change and delete files in my Google Drive. It works only on my library folder: the files in it, with their names, sizes and modification times.

How the data is used

Only to keep that folder the same on Google Drive and on my computers. Nothing is profiled or used for advertising, and nothing is used to train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

Where the data is stored

Copies of the files are kept on my own computers. The access token that rclone needs is stored in rclone's configuration file on my computer. I run no server for this client and keep no other copy.

Sharing

No Google user data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone. The client's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be withdrawn at any time under Third-party apps & services in the Google Account. Deleting the rclone remote on my computer removes the stored token, and deleting the local folder removes the local copies.

Changes

Any change to this policy will appear on this page with a new effective date.
