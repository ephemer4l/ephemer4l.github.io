Strategic Advice

TEV-DAAD Germany Scholarship

Eligibility Criteria

  • T.C. citizen and under 35 years old.
  • Min. GPA: 3.0/4.0 or 76.5/100.
  • Degree from a YÖK-affiliated university in Turkey.

Language Requirement

English: TOEFL IBT (79-93) or IELTS Academic (6.5).
Exemption: No test needed if your Bachelor's was 100% in English and you are applying to an English Master's program (must also be accepted by the university).

Required Documents

  • TEV and DAAD Application Forms
  • CV (English, 1 page preferred)
  • English Transcript
  • Two (2) Letters of Recommendation (English)
  • Family financial documents from e-Devlet
  • Vukuatlı Nüfus Kayıt Örneği & ID Card Copy
